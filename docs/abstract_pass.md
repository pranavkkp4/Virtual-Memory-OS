# Abstract (Progress-Report Framing)

This progress report (as of April 4, 2026) describes a virtual-memory simulator and benchmarking framework for studying page replacement and frame allocation under multiprogrammed workloads. The simulator and experiment harness are implemented, including FIFO, LRU, NRU, Second Chance, and WSClock replacement; global, local, and proportional allocation; and workload drivers with both synthetic generators and trace-driven replay. The pipeline automates parameter sweeps, records page-fault and slowdown metrics (via isolation replays), and produces CSV/JSON summaries and plots. We have exercised the framework with synthetic and trace-driven runs and completed preliminary experiments, including memory-pressure sweeps near the aggregate working-set-size boundary. Before final submission, we are expanding the evaluation (additional workloads/traces, repetitions, and sensitivity checks), finalizing the core figures/tables and interpretation, and polishing the reproducible artifact packaging and documentation.
